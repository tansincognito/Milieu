# Discount and Commitment Policy — Enterprise Tier

Owner: Riley Chen (CRO)
Last updated: 2026-10-16

## Policy

Effective immediately: no discount greater than **15%** off list price may be offered on
the Enterprise tier without CRO sign-off. This applies to net-new deals and renewals
alike. Enterprise tier itself requires a minimum **annual** commitment — quarterly terms
are not available at this tier, full stop.

This is a hard policy, not a negotiating guideline. Two deals last quarter went out at
22% and 18% without sign-off, and both are why this is being written down formally
instead of left as verbal guidance.

## Rationale

Enterprise-tier margins don't support anything beyond 15% once support and onboarding
cost are factored in. Quarterly terms at this tier create churn risk that the CS team
flagged independently — an annual floor is the fix.
