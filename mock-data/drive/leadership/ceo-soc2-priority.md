# Strategic Priority — SOC 2 Type II

Owner: Morgan Ellis (CEO)
Last updated: 2026-10-12

## Priority

SOC 2 Type II certification is the **#1 company priority for Q1 2027**. This is a P0,
above every other roadmap item including in-flight customer commitments. Three of our
five largest renewal opportunities next year — including Acme — have told their security
teams SOC 2 Type II is a hard requirement to continue, not a nice-to-have.

Every team's Q1 planning should treat this as the constraint everything else fits around,
not one priority among several. If Product or Engineering finds a roadmap conflict, it
comes back to me before anything is deprioritized quietly.

## Rationale

We've been SOC 2 Type I for three years. Type II requires a 6-12 month observation
window with no gaps, which means the clock has to start now to land certification before
next year's renewal cycle. This is a start-the-clock decision, not a scoping one — the
audit firm engagement begins this quarter regardless of what else is in flight.
