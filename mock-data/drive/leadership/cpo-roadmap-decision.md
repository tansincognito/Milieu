# Roadmap Decision — Multi-Region Deployment

Owner: Jordan Vega (CPO)
Last updated: 2026-10-14

## Decision

Multi-region deployment support ships in **Q2 2027**, EU region first. This is a committed
roadmap decision, not a target — Legal has already signed the EU data processing addendum
contingent on this date, and two prospects in the pipeline (Initech among them) are
conditioning their contract on it.

Only the EU region is committed for Q2. US-West and APAC are explicitly **not** in this
commitment and have no date yet — Product should not let either get implied to a
customer as part of the Q2 delivery.

## Rationale

This is a direct consequence of the CEO's SOC 2 Type II priority: EU customers are asking
for data residency as part of their own compliance posture, and multi-region is the
prerequisite. Sales has been citing "early 2027" informally for two quarters; this
decision is what actually locks the date and the scope (EU only).
