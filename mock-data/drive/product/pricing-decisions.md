# Pricing Decisions

Owner: Sam Rivera (Product Manager)

## Decision: Salesforce connector is a paid add-on

The Salesforce connector is **not** included on the Enterprise plan. It ships as a paid
add-on, priced separately, regardless of plan tier. Sales should not represent it as
bundled with Enterprise in customer conversations — this has come up before and it's a
support/billing headache when it's promised for free.
